I am a PhD candidate in Bioengineering at the University of Pennsylvania, where I work in the AI in Biomedical Imaging Lab. My research develops deep generative models for normative modelling and anomaly detection in neuroimaging radiomic data, with the goal of identifying image-derived biomarkers for neurodegenerative and neuropsychological diseases.

Before my PhD, I held research and data roles at Biogen, Harvard Medical School, and Regeneron Pharmaceuticals, working on medical image analysis pipelines, CNN-based research integrity tools, and disease quantification in pre-clinical imaging.

I hold an M.S. in Biomedical Engineering from Columbia University (GPA 3.93) and a B.Tech from Manipal Institute of Technology (Gold Medalist). My interests span representation learning, probabilistic generative models, and scalable AI systems for biomedical data.
